How is this calculated?

A poly bag is flat, so a product with height (thickness) uses up bag in both directions:

  • Width: the bag wraps around the front, back and both sides, so it needs product width + height.
  • Length: the bag has to pinch shut below the product (half the height) and again above it (the other half) so the mouth lies flat. So it needs product length + height.
  • On top of that: your extra room on both, and 1 cm closing room on the length so the mouth lies flat and the adhesive flap folds over and seals fully.

Example: a 9 × 9 × 9 cm cube needs 9 + 9 = 18 cm of width and 9 + 9 = 18 cm of length before extra room, not 9 × 9. Poly bag film is treated as not stretching: a bag that only fits by stretching tears or pops its seal in transit.
The app then picks the smallest bag in your list that covers both, trying the product both ways round (portrait and turned sideways). Soft item (clothing) counts the height at half, because folded fabric squashes and moulds to the bag, and switches the tight-fit rule off. Boxes and rigid items should leave it off.
If the smallest bag that fits leaves less than ½ inch spare in either direction, it's flagged as tight and the next size up is recommended. The 2 inch flap is on top of the bag length, the same way your supplier quotes sizes.
