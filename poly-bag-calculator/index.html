How is this calculated?

A bag lying flat has to wrap around the product, so the thickness eats into its width.
Width needed = product width + height + extra room.
Length needed = product length + extra room.
The app then picks the smallest bag in your list that covers both, trying the product both ways round (portrait and turned sideways). The 2 inch flap is on top of the bag length, the same way your supplier quotes sizes.
