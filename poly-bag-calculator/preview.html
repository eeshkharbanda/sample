How is this calculated?

A poly bag is flat, so a product with height (thickness) uses up bag in both directions:

  • Width: the bag wraps around the front, back and both sides, so it needs product width + height.
  • Length: the bag pinches shut below the product (half the height) and again above it (the other half) so the mouth lies flat. So it needs product length + height.
  • Plus your extra room on both, and 1 cm closing room on the length so the adhesive flap folds over and seals fully.

Example: a 9 × 9 × 9 cm cube needs 18 cm of width and 18 cm of length before extra room, not 9 × 9. Bag film is treated as not stretching: a bag that only fits by stretching tears or pops its seal in transit.

The app picks the smallest bag in your list that covers both, trying the product upright and turned sideways. If that bag leaves less than ½ inch spare in either direction, it's flagged as tight and the next size up is recommended. Soft item (clothing) counts height at half and switches the tight-fit rule off. The 2 inch flap is on top of the bag length, the way suppliers quote sizes.
